Spot a slow week before it hurts

Four KPI sparklines sit above a 30-day sales trend and an invoice-status donut — refreshed the moment a bill is punched at any counter.

  • Today invoices, average bill value, net cash flow, receivables vs payables
  • 30-day sales area chart with daily invoice-count overlay
  • Invoice-status donut — paid / overdue / sent / draft by invoiced value
  • Period switcher: today, this week, month or custom range

03 · Collections & Leaders Money In / Money Out

Know exactly where the money is

Daily cash-in vs cash-out bars, the UPI / bank / card / cash mix, and ranked lists of debtors, best sellers and top customers — the owner's morning read.

  • Cash-flow bars catch a squeeze weeks before it lands
  • Payment-mode donut shows how collections actually arrive
  • Top debtors with days-overdue chips and a one-tap Collect action
  • Top products & customers by units, revenue and dues

04 · GST Invoicing Invoice Register

Every bill, searchable and exportable

The invoice register keeps every sale organised — filter by status, date or party, collect pending payments in one tap, and hand your CA a clean export.

  • Paid / sent / partial / overdue status chips on every invoice
  • Filter by date range, customer or amount — search invoice numbers
  • Bulk export to CSV / ZIP for GSTR filing and CA handover
  • Record payments against invoices without leaving the list
app.truerp.in/invoices
05 · Invoice Builder Tax-Correct Billing

A GST-perfect invoice in under a minute

Pick a party, add line items, and the builder handles CGST / SGST / IGST splits by place of supply, freight and discounts — ready to print, share or e-invoice.

  • Auto CGST / SGST / IGST split from party GSTIN & state
  • HSN codes, line-level discounts, transport & other charges
  • Split tender — part cash, part UPI, part credit on one bill
  • Branded A4 PDF, WhatsApp share and e-invoice (IRN + QR)
app.truerp.in/invoices/create

  • Barcode & weighing-scale input with PLU support

  • UPI / cash / card / split tender with live balance & thermal print
