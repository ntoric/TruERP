FAQ

Questions before you subscribe

Do I need to install a server or buy any hardware?

No. TruERP is a fully managed cloud service — sign in from any laptop, desktop or tablet browser, or install our lightweight macOS / Windows counter app. It works with your existing barcode scanner and thermal or A4 printer.

How does the free migration from Tally, Vyapar or Excel work?

Export your data (or share your backup file with our onboarding team) and we import your customers, suppliers, products with HSN codes, and opening balances into your TruERP workspace — usually within 24 hours.

What happens at the counter if the shop internet drops briefly?

Our macOS and Windows desktop app serves the interface locally on your counter machine, so screens stay snappy even on slow connections and sync with the cloud automatically.

Is my business data safe and can I export it anytime?

Every workspace is isolated, served over encrypted TLS, and backed up daily. You own your books — export all invoices, ledgers and GSTR reports to CSV, Excel or ZIP at any time with one click.

Are GST updates and new features really included?

Yes. There are no paid version upgrades or annual maintenance contracts. Every GST rule update and new module rolls out to your cloud workspace automatically.
